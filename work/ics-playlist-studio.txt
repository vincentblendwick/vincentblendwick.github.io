Sole designer and engineer.</p><p class="cfc3f5i">Responsible for the generation model, the studio interface, the Ride Mode split, and the deployment.</p></div></div></div><div class="c79c925"><p class="cyt0ltr">INTERACTION MODEL</p><div class="cpnsihb"></div><div class="c1e9z0dw"><div class="cl0rb5y"><p class="cyt0ltr">01  <span class="cgf04gs lp-raute" aria-hidden="true">⬩</span>  HUMAN</p><p class="c1nfvdop">Stays the author.</p><div class="c4tsxvs"><div class="c1m79svi"><p class="c15nhtck">—</p><p class="cdlxoy1">Sets the ride intent: type, structure, duration, the cues that make it theirs.</p></div><div class="c1m79svi"><p class="c15nhtck">—</p><p class="cdlxoy1">Edits every generated block. The draft is a starting point, not a verdict.</p></div><div class="c1m79svi"><p class="c15nhtck">—</p><p class="cdlxoy1">Decides when the set is finished and the ride begins.</p></div></div></div><div class="cl0rb5y"><p class="cyt0ltr">02  <span class="cgf04gs lp-raute" aria-hidden="true">⬩</span>  HANDOFF</p><p class="c1nfvdop">Two seams, not one.</p><div class="c4tsxvs"><div class="c1m79svi"><p class="c15nhtck">—</p><p class="cdlxoy1">Generation → authoring: the system returns a complete but fully editable draft. It never publishes on its own.</p></div><div class="c1m79svi"><p class="c15nhtck">—</p><p class="cdlxoy1">Authoring → Ride Mode: crossing this seam is a deliberate act, and it removes every editing capability.</p></div><div class="c1m79svi"><p class="c15nhtck">—</p><p class="cdlxoy1">Ride Mode offers no way to modify or delete.</p></div></div></div><div class="cl0rb5y"><p class="cyt0ltr">03  <span class="cgf04gs lp-raute" aria-hidden="true">⬩</span>  AGENT</p><p class="c1nfvdop">Proposes structure, keeps time, commits nothing.</p><div class="c4tsxvs"><div class="c1m79svi"><p class="c15nhtck">—</p><p class="cdlxoy1">Maps ride structure to track selection and block timing.</p></div><div class="c1m79svi"><p class="c15nhtck">—</p><p class="cdlxoy1">Produces a coherent whole rather than a list of suggestions.</p></div><div class="c1m79svi"><p class="c15nhtck">—</p><p class="cdlxoy1">Holds the beat for the whole ride, so the room stays in time and the instructor calls it instead of counting it.</p></div><div class="c1m79svi"><p class="c15nhtck">—</p><p class="cdlxoy1">Can change nothing during a ride. Keeping time is the only authority it has there.</p></div></div></div></div></div><div class="cmydtzn"><p class="cyt0ltr">AUTHORITY MODEL</p><p class="c10kyhdx">Two modes, and what each one puts within reach.</p><p class="cfc3f5i">An instructor has two jobs that never happen at the same time: building a session, and running it in front of a room. So the app has two modes, and they don’t have the same powers.</p><div class="c5sfa80"><div class="cpnsihb"></div><div class="c1noekgd"><p class="c1z0qdhj">VIEW</p><p class="cvmdh0q">AUTHORING</p><div class="cqt6aqx"><p class="cczl44k">✓</p><p class="c1qbd9kl">Available</p></div><p class="cvmdh0q">RIDE</p><div class="cqt6aqx"><p class="cczl44k">✓</p><p class="c1qbd9kl">Available</p></div></div><div class="cpnsihb"></div><div class="c1noekgd"><p class="c1z0qdhj">NAVIGATE</p><p class="cvmdh0q">AUTHORING</p><div class="cqt6aqx"><p class="cczl44k">✓</p><p class="c1qbd9kl">Available</p></div><p class="cvmdh0q">RIDE</p><div class="cqt6aqx"><p class="cczl44k">✓</p><p class="c1qbd9kl">Available</p></div></div><div class="cpnsihb"></div><div class="c1noekgd"><p class="c1z0qdhj">MODIFY</p><p class="cvmdh0q">AUTHORING</p><div class="cqt6aqx"><p class="cczl44k">✓</p><p class="c1qbd9kl">Available</p></div><p class="cvmdh0q">RIDE</p><div class="cqt6aqx"><p class="cxcizlt">—</p><p class="c1cldu5v">not offered</p></div></div><div class="cpnsihb"></div><div class="c1noekgd"><p class="c1z0qdhj">DELETE</p><p class="cvmdh0q">AUTHORING</p><div class="cqt6aqx"><p class="cczl44k">✓</p><p class="c1qbd9kl">Available</p></div><p class="cvmdh0q">RIDE</p><div class="cqt6aqx"><p class="cxcizlt">—</p><p class="c1cldu5v">not offered</p></div></div><div class="cpnsihb"></div></div><p class="c10jvbq1">What the authoring mode and Ride Mode each put within reach</p><p class="c1nfvdop">A confirmation dialog still allows an unsafe action. It just adds a step in front of it. In Ride Mode, editing isn’t hidden behind a warning — the mode doesn’t put it in reach at all.</p><p class="cfc3f5i">The system can suggest changes to a session. Nothing goes in until the instructor accepts it.</p></div><div class="cmydtzn"><p class="cyt0ltr">IN THE STUDIO</p><p class="c10kyhdx">Ride Mode,
read-only on purpose.</p><p class="cfc3f5i">Ride Mode on the phone, the way instructors use it during a class. No edit, no save, no delete — the mode doesn’t put them in reach.</p></div><div class="c4tsxvs"><p class="cundhys">BECAUSE</p><p class="c1bpv1dj">During a class, an accidental touch isn’t a minor annoyance — it’s a visible failure in front of a room of people who paid to be there, at the one moment the instructor can’t look down and recover.</p></div><div class="c4tsxvs"><p class="cvmdh0q">WHAT IT COSTS</p><p class="c1bpv1dj">An instructor who notices a mistake mid-ride genuinely cannot fix it in place. They have to leave Ride Mode to do it.</p></div></div><div class="cpnsihb"></div><div class="cxsyij2"><div class="c5sfa80"><p class="c1nfvdop">Accept that cost rather than softening the boundary.</p></div><div class="c4tsxvs"><p class="cundhys">BECAUSE</p><p class="c1bpv1dj">Leaving a mode is a conscious act; a mis-swipe is not.</p></div><div class="c4tsxvs"><p class="cvmdh0q">WHAT IT COSTS</p><p class="c1bpv1dj">The recovery path is slower than it could be, and that is the point.</p></div></div><div class="cpnsihb"></div></div></div><div class="cvvdno"><div class="cwbixku"><p class="c1qejeld">WHAT COULD GO WRONG</p><p class="c10kyhdx">Mid-ride, under load, the instructor taps the wrong thing and changes the session.</p><div class="c1e9z0dw"><div class="cl0rb5y"><p class="cundhys">AUTHORITY</p><p class="c1bpv1dj">Changing a session is an authoring decision, never a ride decision.</p></div><div class="cl0rb5y"><p class="cundhys">GUARDRAIL</p><p class="c1bpv1dj">Ride Mode doesn’t put modify or delete in reach.</p></div></div></div></div><div class="cytdwd2"><p class="cyt0ltr">RESULT</p><div class="c1e9z0dw"><div class="cl0rb5y"><div class="cpnsihb"></div><p class="cvmdh0q">IN USE</p><h2 class="cw4fkjs">Prep collapsed to seconds</h2><p class="c1bpv1dj">Playlist, movements and live delivery in one — each track cued about five seconds before it plays, at the same quality every ride.</p></div><div class="cl0rb5y"><div class="cpnsihb"></div><p class="cvmdh0q">STATUS</p><h2 class="cw4fkjs">Live</h2><p class="c1bpv1dj">The screens above show the real deployment, in read-only Ride Mode.</p></div></div></div><div class="c13kapa7" id="contact-m"><p class="c1z0qdhj">REACH ME</p><h2 class="c1gvx4db">Say hi.</h2><div class="c53rnqe"><a class="cisso4z" href="mailto:bryan@vincentblendwick.com">bryan@vincentblendwick.com</a><a class="cisso4z" href="/">vincentblendwick.com</a></div><div class="c1ots0p6"><p class="c6pkxc9">vincent &amp; blendwick
