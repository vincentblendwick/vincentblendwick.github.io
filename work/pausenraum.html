Pausenraum, and where the agent has to step back

Pausenraum is for people whose job is wearing them down. It starts with a conversation, not a form. That conversation is the reason to use an agent at all — and the most dangerous place to use one, because the person on the other side might not be okay.

START ⬩ WRITE IT ALL OUT ⬩ BATTERY ⬩ REPORT ⬩ GERMAN PRODUCT, REAL SCREENS

TIDYING UP ⬩ SCROLL

Everything in one heap. Then one thing at a time.

First you write out everything that is going on. Then Pausenraum sorts it with you, piece by piece, until it stands on its own. Scroll and watch the heap settle.

SCROLL: THE STACK FALLS INTO A HEAP ⬩ KEEP SCROLLING: EVERY PIECE IS LAID DOWN FLAT, SORTED BY COLOUR, WARM LEFT OF THE DOT, COOL RIGHT

CONTEXT

Most job platforms start with the job. People who are struggling at work usually start somewhere else: tired, angry, not sure whether to stay or go. A job search at that point is the wrong first step.

So Pausenraum takes the pressure off first — write it all out, check your battery, take one small step — and only then helps with staying, switching internally or searching.

It works the way systemic coaching does: advising without giving advice. It places the people around your work, asks circular questions, and separates what lies with the structure from what you can change.

An agent that talks to someone under stress has to know, before anything else, when to stop talking.

Letting off steam, staying and switching internally are free. Only the job search costs money, because help shouldn’t start with a paywall.

CRISIS PATH ⬩ ONE TAP AWAY

Immediate help with phone numbers, not a chat: crisis lines, the out-of-hours doctor, emergency services. Breathing and calming sounds alongside.

The crisis path doesn’t depend on the agent. If the conversation fails, the numbers still work.

Every screen carries the same line at the bottom: if things are really bad right now, help is here. It leads to real numbers, sorted by urgency, with one big button each.

THE END IS PART OF THE PRODUCT

When it works, Pausenraum says so — and then lets go.

A new job gets a moment of its own: “Geschafft.” After that comes a clean exit and a good start, and the subscription ends by itself after onboarding. The data stays 30 days, then everything is deleted.

DECISIONS

No community.

BECAUSE

People who are already low can pull each other further down. A feed rewards the worst day.

WHAT IT COSTS

No network effects, and no content from users to fill the product.

Relief before jobs.

BECAUSE

WHAT IT COSTS
