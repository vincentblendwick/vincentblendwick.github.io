People who are struggling at work usually start somewhere else: tired, angry, not sure whether to stay or go. A job search at that point is the wrong first step.</p><p class="c1q4pcov">So Pausenraum takes the pressure off first — write it all out, check your battery, take one small step — and only then helps with staying, switching internally or searching.</p><p class="c1q4pcov">It works the way systemic coaching does: advising without giving advice. It places the people around your work, asks circular questions, and separates what lies with the structure from what you can change.</p><p class="cdfzawv">An agent that talks to someone under stress has to know, before anything else, when to stop talking.</p><p class="c1q4pcov">Letting off steam, staying and switching internally are free. Only the job search costs money, because help shouldn’t start with a paywall.</p></div></div><div class="cfzzbgl"><div class="c70s8lp"><p class="c1hh1zyv">MY ROLE</p></div><div class="c1tvjzxp"><p class="c1q4pcov">Concept, product design and build.
