THE LENS

Autonomy isn’t a feature. It’s a design decision about authority

When I design a system that works alongside people, my first question isn’t what it can do. It’s what it’s allowed to do. What can it observe, what can it recommend, and what can it act on by itself? What stays a human call? And is that line just suggested by the interface, or built into the system so nobody crosses it by accident?
